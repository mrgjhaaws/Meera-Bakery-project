    Our story

    Baked from a home kitchen, with a bakery's heart

    Meera Bakery started as a small home kitchen in Bengaluru, driven by one simple belief — food made with love tastes better. Every morning before sunrise, our bakers are at work, mixing, proving, and baking so that everything on your table is truly fresh.

    We keep our menu small on purpose: a short list of things we've perfected, made the same honest way every single day.
